Six years of orders, closed and counted — the house held its line.

Across seventy-five months the storefront turned twenty-five thousand orders into gross revenue, at an average basket a shade above one hundred and twenty-five dollars. Growth returned in the final stretch: the trailing year closed up nearly thirteen percent, with January 2026 the strongest month on record.
